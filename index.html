NIXL on ROCm: nightly results

Each night the rocm-nixl wheels are built from the branches below and tested in a clean container and in QEMU guests with an emulated GPU. This page shows how each branch has fared, night by night.

The transfer rates come from emulated devices on shared GitHub runners. Each one is the bytes a check moved divided by the time the whole check took, set-up included. They are here to show trends, not to measure performance.
